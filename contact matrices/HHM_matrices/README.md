# **Contact Matrices - Household Members**

The contact calculation process is described here. 

Each file contains an age-structured matrix corresponding to mean contacts reported by household members of acute gastroenteritis (AGE) and 
acute respiratory infection (ARI) cases for two survey time points: index survey (corresponding to the AGE/ARI case’s most sick day) and 1-week follow-up survey.  

The row names correspond to the respondent group (household members of ill individuals), and the column names represent the contact group. 
Respondent and contact ages are grouped into 6 age groups: <5 years, 5-17 years, 18-29 years, 30-44 years, 45-64 years, 65+ years). 

The names of the files follow the same naming pattern: ‘HHM_survey_matrix.xlsx’, where survey corresponds to the survey time point.
